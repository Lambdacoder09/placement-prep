## 1 · What the three tiers mean

Every chapter has the same four blocks. You climb them in order, inside the chapter.

  [**Tier**], [**Companies**], [**What the questions feel like**], [**Time / Q**],

  text(weight: "bold", fill: warmcol)[0 Warm-up],
  [nobody --- it is you],
  [Bare mechanics. One idea per question. You are building the reflex, not being tested.],
  [30 s],

  text(weight: "bold", fill: t1col)[1 Service],
  [TCS NQT, Accenture, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, Cognizant],
  [Direct formula use. Light two-step word problems. Clean numbers. Speed beats depth.],
  [45--75 s],

  text(weight: "bold", fill: t2col)[2 SE Asia],
  [Grab, Sea/Shopee, GIC, DBS, Agoda, SCB, LINE MAN, Razer],
  [Two or three steps, mixed topics in one question. Fares, orders, FX, logistics. Often no options given.],
  [90--120 s],

  text(weight: "bold", fill: t3col)[3 Product],
  [Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, D. E. Shaw, Adobe, Uber],
  [Needs an insight, not recall. Counting arguments, invariants, expectation, bounds, estimation. Often asked aloud in an interview.],
  [3--6 min],
)

Target the tier that matches the companies you are applying to --- but always work Tier 1
first, even for a product-company interview. The Tier 3 questions are built on the Tier 1
and Tier 2 ones that come before them in the same chapter.

## 2 · The five rules

#table(
  columns: (auto, 1fr),
  inset: (x: 5pt, y: 4.5pt),
  stroke: 0.4pt + rule,

  [**1**], [**Never read a solution before you have attempted the problem.** Give it a full,
  honest try with a pen. A solution you read before trying teaches you nothing --- it only
  feels like learning.],

  [**2**], [If you are stuck for more than the tier's time limit, stop. Write down **where**
  you got stuck. Then read the solution and find that exact line.],

  [**3**], [Work every practice set on paper, timed, with the answer key covered. Mark it
  only after the timer ends.],

  [**4**], [Redo every question you got wrong --- three days later, from a blank page. Not
  the same day. Three days later.],

  [**5**], [Do the arithmetic in your head wherever the appendix tables let you. The
  calculator is not there on test day, and neither is long division time.],
)

## 3 · The revision cards

Each chapter ends with a one-page **revision card**: every formula, every shortcut, and the
top five traps.

#table(
  columns: (auto, 1fr),
  inset: (x: 5pt, y: 4pt),
  stroke: none,
  [**While studying**], [Ignore it. Learn from the worked examples instead.],
  [**After finishing a chapter**], [Cover the card. Write it out from memory. Compare. The gaps are your revision list.],
  [**Last week before a test**], [Read only the 24 cards --- about 40 minutes for the whole book. Then attempt one mock paper per day from Chapter 24.],
  [**Last hour**], [Cards of your three weakest chapters, plus the speed-arithmetic tables at the back. Nothing else.],
)

## 4 · The 12-week plan

Two chapters a week. About 90 minutes a day, six days a week. Day 7 is the checkpoint.

## 5 · The 4-week crash plan

Use this only if your test is inside a month. It skips nothing --- it just cuts the
practice volume. About 3 hours a day.

#table(
  columns: (auto, auto, 1fr, 1fr),

  [**Week**], [**Chapters**], [**Daily target**], [**Checkpoint (day 7)**],

  [1], [1--8],    [Worked examples of Tier 1 and Tier 2 only. Skip Tier 3 for now. Two chapters a day, days 1--4; practice sets on days 5--6.], [Mixed sets of Ch 1--8, timed at 60 s/Q],
  [2], [9--14],   [Same method. Add the Tier 3 examples of Ch 9, 10 and 11 if you are targeting a product company.], [Revision cards of Ch 1--14, written from memory],
  [3], [15--22],  [Reasoning and DI. One full puzzle set and one full DI set every day, always timed.], [Mixed sets of Ch 15--22],
  [4], [23, 24],  [Data sufficiency on days 1--2. Then one mock paper per day, days 3--6, scored properly.], [Redo the two lowest-scoring papers. Read all 24 revision cards.],
)

---
⚠️ **TRAP**

The crash plan works only if you **time everything from day one**. Untimed practice will
make you feel ready and then fail you on the clock.
