## Rules specific to this book
- Every behavioural answer is shown in **STAR** form with the four parts LABELLED.
- Give a WEAK answer and a STRONG answer side by side, and name exactly what changed.
  Use #trap[] for the weak version and #sol[] for the strong one.
- Never write a script to memorise. Give a STRUCTURE plus a filled example, and say clearly
  that the student must substitute their own real experience. Say this explicitly and often.
- Be honest about hard questions (salary expectations, bond/service agreements, gaps in
  study, backlogs, rejections, notice period). Give the real strategy, not a platitude.
- Puzzles chapters follow the normal #ex/#sol/#ans format with full reasoning.
- Do not coach anyone to lie. Where a student has a genuine weakness (a gap, a backlog, a
  low CGPA), teach them to frame it truthfully and move to evidence of improvement.
